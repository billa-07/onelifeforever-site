One Life Forever

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game One Life Forever ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: ibtsham@rajpootholding.com.

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We store a player name you choose and the country you confirm.
  • We never ask for location permission and never use GPS. The world map knows your country only, never a city or an exact place.
  • Your player name, family name, country flag, days survived, grave and last words are shown to other players.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (Google Play, RevenueCat) are handled by those companies.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the game (Profile, Settings, Delete account) or by email: Delete your account.

1. What we collect

Player name (you choose it) and country (you confirm it once)To show you on the world map, on graves and on leaderboards.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. The game has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

2. What other players can see

Your player name, country flag, days survived, rank, your grave (zone, cause of death, last words, flowers) and the whispers or curses you send as a ghost. Last words are public: they are carved on the grave on the world map, where every player can read them. Your family name, its total and best days, its number of generations and whether it is a hardcore family are shown on the family leaderboard. A death card is shared only if you choose to share it. Your keepsakes, Legacy details and login streak are shown only to you. Your email address, phone number and device are never shown to anyone.

Text you write (name, last words, whispers) is filtered and can be reported, hidden or replaced by us.

3. Companies that process data for us

  • Supabase: database, login and server functions (hosting of the data above).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, Google Play Billing, Play Integrity, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Google AdMob: ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data, and may show personalised ads depending on your choices and region. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation. It receives an app user id and purchase records.
  • SMS provider used by Supabase Auth: receives your phone number only to send the verification code.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. A grave stays on the world map after death: that is the game. When you delete your account (in the game or by email) we delete your data as described on Delete your account. Our own purchase records are deleted with the account; Google Play and RevenueCat keep theirs under their policies.

One exception, for at most 24 hours: if you delete your account while your character is in mourning (the 24 hours after a death), the salted hash of your device identifier is kept until the moment your heir could have risen, and then deleted. This prevents skipping the mourning by making a new account on the same phone. The hash cannot be turned back into your device id and nothing else about you is kept with it.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Players cannot read each other's private data; only the server changes player state. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, including objecting to processing and complaining to your data protection authority. Write to ibtsham@rajpootholding.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
