One Life Forever

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game One Life Forever ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: ibtsamrajpoot46@gmail.com.

In short

  • You sign in with Google. We store a player name you choose and the country you confirm.
  • We never ask for location permission and never use GPS. The world map knows your country only, never a city or an exact place.
  • Your player name, country flag, days survived, grave and last words are shown to other players.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (Google Play, RevenueCat) are handled by those companies.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time: Delete your account.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Google account id and email address (from Sign in with Google)To log you in and keep your one life tied to one account.
Player name (you choose it) and country (you confirm it once)To show you on the world map, on graves and on leaderboards.
Phone number, only if the game asks you to verify by SMSTo stop one person from creating many accounts.
A device identifier, stored on our server only as a salted hashTo enforce "one life" per device and prevent cheating.
Play Integrity result from GoogleTo check that the game is genuine and not modified.
Game data: day, HP, loot, perks, runs and their input logs, death record, last words, ghost actions (whispers, curses, pledges), flowers, appealsTo run the game. The server replays every run to decide the result.
Purchases and what you own (Soul Shards, cosmetics, No-Ads, season pass)To give you what you bought and keep it.
Rewarded-ad confirmationsTo grant the ad reward you chose.
Push notification token, if you allow notificationsTo tell you when your runs are ready.
Reports and blocks you sendTo moderate names, whispers, curses and last words.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. The game has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

2. What other players can see

Your player name, country flag, days survived, rank, your grave (zone, cause of death, last words, flowers) and the whispers or curses you send as a ghost. Your email address, phone number and device are never shown to anyone.

Text you write (name, last words, whispers) is filtered and can be reported, hidden or replaced by us.

3. Companies that process data for us

  • Supabase: database, login and server functions (hosting of the data above).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, Google Play Billing, Play Integrity, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Google AdMob: ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data, and may show personalised ads depending on your choices and region. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation. It receives an app user id and purchase records.
  • SMS provider used by Supabase Auth: receives your phone number only to send the verification code.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. A grave stays on the world map after death: that is the game. When you ask for deletion we delete or anonymise your data as described on Delete your account. Purchase records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Players cannot read each other's private data; only the server changes player state. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, including objecting to processing and complaining to your data protection authority. Write to ibtsamrajpoot46@gmail.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
