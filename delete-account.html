Delete your account and data

One Life Forever · Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can delete your One Life Forever account and the data linked to it at any time: inside the game, or by email if you no longer have the game installed.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE and confirm.

The account and its data are deleted from our servers at once and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

By email (if you cannot use the game)

  1. Send an email to ibtsham@rajpootholding.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Send it from the Google account you use in the game, or include that email address and your player name, so we can find the right account.
  3. We may reply once to confirm it is really you. We delete the account within 30 days of the confirmed request.

What is deleted

  • Your login (Google account id and email address) and your phone number, if you verified one.
  • Your player: name, country, day, HP, loot, perks, last words, death record.
  • Your family (Bloodline): family name, every character, their last words and keepsakes, Legacy level, login streak.
  • Your grave on the world map and your entries on the leaderboards.
  • Your runs and run logs, ghost actions (whispers, curses, pledges), ghost score, reports, blocks and appeals.
  • Your Soul Shards, cosmetics, season progress and push notification token.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records in our own database are deleted with the account. Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own records of your purchases under their policies. Deleting the account does not refund purchases.
  • The salted hash of your device identifier is kept only if you delete the account while your character is in mourning (the 24 hours after a death): then it is kept until the moment your heir could have risen, at most 24 hours, and deleted after that. This stops a new account on the same phone from skipping the mourning. The hash cannot be turned back into your device id, and nothing else about you is kept with it.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Data held by Google (Google Play purchases, AdMob advertising data) is controlled by Google; manage it in your Google account.

Delete only some data

You can also ask us to delete only part of your data, for example your last words, your phone number or your grave, without deleting the account. Write to the same address and say what to remove.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your family, your graves and everything you own in the game are removed and cannot be restored.
