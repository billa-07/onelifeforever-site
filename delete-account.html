Delete your account and data

One Life Forever · Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your One Life Forever account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need to have the game installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ibtsamrajpoot46@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Send it from the Google account you use in the game, or include that email address and your player name, so we can find the right account.
  3. We may reply once to confirm it is really you. We delete the account within 30 days of the confirmed request.

What is deleted

  • Your login (Google account id and email address) and your phone number, if you verified one.
  • Your player: name, country, day, HP, loot, perks, last words, death record.
  • Your grave on the world map and your entries on the leaderboards.
  • Your runs and run logs, ghost actions (whispers, curses, pledges), ghost score, reports, blocks and appeals.
  • Your Soul Shards, cosmetics, season progress and push notification token.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (order id, product, date) are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, without your game profile.
  • The salted hash of your device identifier may be kept for up to 90 days to prevent abuse of the one-life rule. It cannot be turned back into your device id.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Data held by Google (Google Play purchases, AdMob advertising data) is controlled by Google; manage it in your Google account.

Delete only some data

You can also ask us to delete only part of your data, for example your last words, your phone number or your grave, without deleting the account. Write to the same address and say what to remove.

Deleting your account is permanent. Your life, your grave and everything you own in the game are removed and cannot be restored.
